SaaS Nation

A library of small business tools for coaches and creators

One account, many focused tools: invoices, proposals, booking, forms, a client list, an email list, digital downloads, a client portal and more. Buy the one tool you need or get All Access.

Visit SaaS Nation

The problem

Coaches and solo businesses often pay for several separate apps to handle basic jobs like booking, invoicing and intake forms.

What we built

  • A Next.js app with per-tool access, so each tool can be bought on its own or as part of All Access
  • Customers are paid through their own Stripe Payment Links; SaaS Nation takes no cut
  • Free no-signup tools and free copy-paste templates that lead visitors to the paid tools
  • Self-hosted on our own server behind a Cloudflare Tunnel

Stack: Next.js · Supabase · Stripe Payment Links · Docker · Cloudflare
