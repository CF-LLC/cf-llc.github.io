FAQ

Care Plan questions

Does it work with a site you did not build?

In most cases, yes. Send me the address and I will take a look first. If I cannot support it well, I will tell you before you pay anything.

What if I need more than one hour of edits?

Extra edits are available at a simple hourly rate that I will quote you up front. Unused edit time does not roll over.

How is this different from Site Care ($49/mo)?

Site Care covers updates, uptime checks and a monthly report. The Care Plan adds monthly off-site backups, security and plugin updates, up to 1 hour of content edits, and priority email support.

How do I sign up?

Send the short form on this page. I will reply by email, look at your site, confirm it is a fit, and set up your first month. There is no long contract.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. Tell me by email and the plan ends at the close of the month you are in. No fees, no hoops.
