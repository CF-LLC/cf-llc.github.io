FAQ

Common questions

Where is my site hosted?

Starter Site and Pro Site include hosting in the monthly fee, so there is nothing extra to set up. If you already have a site and host, Site Care covers updates and uptime checks for it.

Can I make edits later?

Yes. Starter Site includes updates as part of the monthly fee: email me what you want changed. Pro Site includes 2 revision rounds during the build, and updates after launch are covered by the monthly fee.

How long does it take?

A Starter Site is typically live within about a week of approving your mockup, as long as I have your text and photos. Pro Sites take longer depending on pages and features; I will give you an estimate up front.

Do I need to be local?

No. Everything is done remotely by email, for small businesses anywhere in the US.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. No payment or commitment to get a homepage mockup.
